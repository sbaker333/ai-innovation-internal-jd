Why this role exists

AI just empowered a new cohort of builders inside LiveRamp.

AI has empowered a new cohort of builders: the commercial team. Sales, customer experience, implementation, SEs, growth strategists, collaboration architects. These users have firsthand knowledge of the things that make our customers' lives difficult, and the barriers that make cleanroom collaboration slow and painful. Previously, these teams were often powerless to build the solutions they needed. The path to fix the issue always required resources from product and engineering, who had no resources to spare.

Now, tools like Claude Code, Cursor, and Workshop.ai have made it possible for non-engineers with domain expertise to prototype real, working software, often in a matter of hours. Real enough to validate ideas, automate manual work, remove friction, consolidate knowledge, and ultimately create more automated and scalable workflows for our customers and our colleagues.

"Right now, we have the capacity to create these solutions. We're missing an established path to do so safely, with structure, guidance, and purpose. This role is the playbook."

Who'd thrive

You'll feel at home in this role if…

  • You can hold a value hypothesis in one hand and a working prototype in the other, and ship the prototype before the hypothesis hardens.
  • You see a flashy demo that nobody uses and feel a small, productive embarrassment.
  • You believe vibe coding is a craft, not a shortcut. Slow is smooth, smooth is fast.
  • You like being the person who sets the guardrails so other people can move faster.
  • You're at home with InfoSec, BT, Product, Engineering, and Sales in the same week.
  • You'd rather teach ten people to build than ship ten tools yourself.
